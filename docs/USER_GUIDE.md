# Calm Garden — User Guide

Calm Garden is a quiet place to breathe, relax and notice. Everything works
offline; nothing you do is sent anywhere.

## Getting around
* Click a tile, or use **Tab / arrow keys** to move and **Enter** or **Space** to choose.
* **Esc** or the **Back** button goes back. **Ctrl+M** turns sound on/off (also the
  speaker button, top corner). **F11** switches fullscreen. **Ctrl+Q** quits.
* During any exercise: **Space** pauses, **N** switches to natural breathing,
  **Finish** ends it. You can always stop - there is no wrong way to feel.

## Activities
* **Breathing** – follow a flower, balloon, ocean waves or a star. Set how long to
  breathe in and out; an optional *rest after breathing out* is available. There is
  never any breath holding. Press *Breathe naturally* at any time.
* **Guided Relaxation** – six short programmes with written steps that move on by
  themselves (or use Previous/Next). Muscle relaxation: tense gently, never hard,
  and skip anything that hurts.
* **Mindfulness** – Five Senses, Mindful Observation, Thought Clouds, Gratitude
  Garden, Calm Moments. Thoughts you type into clouds are never saved.
* **Biofeedback (Simulated)** – shows what biofeedback looks like. There are no
  sensors: the graphs follow the breathing guide, your mouse/keyboard and your own
  rating. It does not measure your body or your feelings.
* **Relaxation Games** – no scores, no timers to beat, no losing. The Zen Garden
  grows a little each time you finish an exercise.
* **Meditation Timer** – choose minutes, sound, chimes and a picture; the controls
  fade away while you sit and return when you move the mouse or press a key.
* **My Progress** – sessions, minutes, favourites and (if you chose to give them)
  your before/after ratings. These are your own impressions; finishing exercises
  does not prove anything about stress levels.

## Settings
* **Display** – language (English/עברית), text size, child-friendly mode (larger
  text, simpler words, shorter sessions, encouraging messages), calm adult style,
  high contrast, reduced motion, fullscreen, picture theme, animation speed.
* **Sound** – master switch, effects, ambient sound (rain, ocean, wind, forest,
  birds, gentle music) and volumes, menu music, narration.
* **Breathing** – default pace, rest, session length.
* **Privacy** – keep or stop recording practice history, ask/don't ask how you
  feel, delete everything.

## For parents and teachers
* No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no internet access, no external links.
* The only stored data are settings and (optional) practice history in a text file
  in the user's data folder. Children's names are never asked for.
* Calm Garden is an educational relaxation tool, not a medical device or a
  substitute for professional care. If a child seems unwell or very worried,
  please talk with them and seek appropriate help.

## Files
* Linux: `~/.local/share/calm_garden/settings.txt`, `progress.txt`
* Windows: `%APPDATA%\CalmGarden\` (or `userdata\` next to the program when a
  file `portable.txt` exists there)
