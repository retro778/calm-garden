Optional sound files
====================
Calm Garden synthesises all of its sounds itself, so this folder may be empty.
To use your own recordings instead, put files with these names here
(.ogg, .mp3 or .wav):

  rain   ocean   wind   forest   birds   music   chime

Use only recordings you are allowed to redistribute (your own, public domain,
CC0, or another licence that permits it) and add attribution to
docs/THIRD_PARTY.md when the licence requires it.
Keep them quiet and without sudden loud parts.
