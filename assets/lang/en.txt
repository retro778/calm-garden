# ---- simulated biofeedback ----
bio.intro = Biofeedback devices use sensors to show body signals as pictures and graphs. Calm Garden has NO sensors, so everything on this screen is a SIMULATION made by the program: it follows the breathing guide, your mouse and keyboard, and your own rating of how calm you feel. It is a way to explore what biofeedback looks like - it does not measure you.

bio.heart = Heart-like waveform
bio.heart.note = demonstration only - not your heart
bio.focus = Focus game
bio.focus.note = game score (simulated)
bio.calm = Calm colours
bio.calm.note = animation based on your rating and the breathing guide - not a measurement
bio.meter.lo = lively
bio.meter.hi = calm
bio.self = How calm do you feel? (your own rating)
bio.done = Remember: everything you saw was a simulation. Your own feelings are what matter.
bio.explain = How the simulation works\n• Breathing rhythm: the line simply follows the breathing guide (your chosen pace). It does not record your breathing.\n• Heart-like waveform: a drawing made from a mathematical formula, to show what such graphs look like. Its speed changes a little with the breathing guide to illustrate an idea called heart-rate variability. It is not your heartbeat.\n• Focus game: rest your mouse pointer inside the slowly moving circle - or simply keep still - and the bar grows. It measures the game, not your brain.\n• Calm colours: the picture changes colour depending on your own rating, the breathing guide, and whether you are clicking quickly. It is an animation, not a measurement of your feelings.\nReal biofeedback needs special sensors and is often used with a trained professional. Calm Garden is an educational tool and not a medical device.
